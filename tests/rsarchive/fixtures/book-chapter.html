The Soul Calendar

Northern Hemisphere

Week 35

Can I in such wise know
Being that, being known,
it find itself again
in soul's creative thrust?
Strength I feel given me in trust
to have a humble self begin to be
a member of the Selfhood of the world.

—Tr. Owen Barfield

Southern Hemisphere

Week 09

Regardless of all individual willing,
telling of summer coming soon,
the Warmth that warms the worlds
my spirit and my soul is filling;
my spirit's penetrating gaze
tells me to lose myself into the light
and, 'Lose thyself to find thyself'
I hear a voice within incessantly recite.

—Tr. Owen Barfield
